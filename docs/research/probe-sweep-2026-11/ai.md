# AI engine side: differential probe findings (brain, platoons, threat, AI queries)

Probes (new): `dev/probes/maps/probe_ai_threat`, `probe_ai_brain`, `probe_ai_platoon`, `probe_ai_build`, `probe_ai_misc`; follow-ups `probe_ai_transport` (transport platoon calls on a live platoon, with `DisbandOnIdle` moved to its own throw-away platoon) and `probe_ai_find` (the `Find*Unit` family asked at t = 3, 8, 20, 35, 60, 100, 520 while intel builds and after it is lost; per-type threat with no trailing nils; ring sums; cell scans; decay start). Real-game logs: `dev/probes/out/probe_ai_*.faf.log`. Our logs are not kept. Map SCMP_009 (1024), army 1 asks, NEUTRAL_CIVILIAN is the enemy.

All five real runs are complete (threat 429 lines, brain 467, platoon 364, build 301; misc complete on the rerun with `CanBuildStructureAt('zzz9999', pos)` disabled because it takes the game down, D30). In misc, ours does not reach `PROBE done`: the engine run aborts two ticks after the last risky call (D43).

Severity: **A** = FAF / RNGAI AI code calls it and ours answers differently; **B** = wrong but
unused or rarely reached; **C** = cosmetic (return shape, error text, ordering).
Usage counts are call sites in `../fa/lua` / RNGAI.
