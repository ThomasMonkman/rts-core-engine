# Intel: radar jamming and contact icons, real game vs ours

Differential probe sweep, 2026-11-15. Real game = FAF's `ForgedAlliance.exe` with FAF's Lua; ours = `target/release/rts-core-engine --headless --no-ai`. Severity: B = gameplay-visible, C = cosmetic / API edge.

Probes (new, under `dev/probes/maps/`): `probe_intel_jam` (`probe.lua` = v3, `probe_v1.lua`, `probe_v2.lua`) and `probe_intel_icons` (`probe.lua` and `ui_probe.lua`). Four real-game runs (jam v1, v2, v3, icons), all complete (`PROBE done`). The only real-game Lua errors were FAF wreckage / commander-unit errors from clearing the start units. Real logs: `dev/probes/out/probe_intel_jam.faf.log` (the v3 run) and `dev/probes/out/probe_intel_icons.faf.log`, with 15 screenshots `dev/probes/out/probe_intel_icons_{radar,seen,after}_{all,west,mid,east,far}.png`. Our logs and screenshots are not kept.

All rows marked fixed are on `fix/probe-sweep-intel` and now on `fix/probe-sweep`; the hashes are those of `fix/probe-sweep`.

Setup: army 1 observes with an enemy-radar-only view (T3 radar `ueb3104` far enough that its omni 200 misses, or T2 radar `ueb3201` plus T2 sonar `ueb3202`); army 2 owns the jammers and targets; all hold fire. Jammers: frigate `ues0103` (`JammerBlips` 5, `JamRadius` 70/70), Sparky `xel0209` (8, 18/18), spy plane `uea0302` (4, Min 10 / Max 25).

## Side findings

Not jamming: our intel came up late — at tick 2 ours had `IsIntelEnabled('Jammer'/'Radar')` false, jammers on at ~21 and the T3 radar's first contact at tick 62, against real true at tick 2 and first contacts at tick 10 (could be the energy start-up; not investigated). Since `4a6db9a` ours is no longer late but 9 ticks early (contacts at tick 1): the 10-tick intel grid of J12, kept ours. In the real game the idle Sparky drifted ~100 units north-east by itself in every run (not investigated).

## What matches
Fake count; fakes absent from `GetUnitsAroundPoint`; jammer's own blip via `GetBlip`; weapons not
shooting fakes (by absence); `GetUnitById` hiding enemy units; artillery targeting a radar-only real
unit; mobile blip kept after the unit dies.
