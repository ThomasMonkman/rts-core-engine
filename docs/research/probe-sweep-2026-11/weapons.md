Our logs are not kept in the repo; the `PROBE` lines are what was diffed.

Probe-writing gotchas found (worth adding to `docs/guides/probing.md`):
- Hooking `wep.OnFire` / `OnGotTarget` on the instance shadows FAF's weapon *state* methods and stops the weapon
  firing in both engines. The hooks here step aside while the state's own method runs (see `hook()` in `probe_wpn_census`).
- Calling `GetBlueprint()` on a shield entity (from a projectile's `OnImpact(‘Shield’, entity)`) ended the real game's run
  silently (log stops, no error). Test `entity.Owner` first.
- The real `DamageArea` / `DamageRing` raise `0 radius specified` and abort the calling thread.
- The real `GetPosition()` returns the same table every call (see finding 5): keep numbers, not the table.
- AIs run for armies 5-8 in the real game's probe sessions.
