# Units, orders, economy, construction, unit API: real game vs ours

Differential probe sweep, 2026-11-14. Real game = FAF's `ForgedAlliance.exe` with FAF's Lua; ours = `target/release/rts-core-engine --headless --no-ai`.

Probes (all new, under `dev/probes/maps/`): `probe_unit_api`, `probe_unit_econ`, `probe_unit_build`, `probe_unit_orders`, `probe_unit_intel`, `probe_unit_misc`; follow-up probes `probe_unit_capture2`, `probe_unit_reclaim2`.
Logs: real `dev/probes/out/probe_unit_*.faf.log`; our logs are not kept. "F" = real, "O" = ours. Tick offsets of 1-3 (ours earlier) are left out as intentional.

Run status after the reruns: econ, intel, orders, misc and build (to `PROBE done`) complete. `probe_unit_api` runs up to its last risky call, `pgen:CanPathTo`, which takes the real game down (`GetAttacker`, disabled in the probe, does too).

Two things were confirmed with small ours-only scripts (not kept): the cancelled-upgrade drain and the engineer aggressive move.

Reading note for economy numbers in G5-G9: both engines carry a background drain from earlier groups (real: energy 32.00 a tick,
mass 0, visible at `F G7 2 prey +1 work 0.000 ... energy 32.00`; ours: mass 4.62 and energy 111.31 a tick, i.e. row 1's two
cancelled / orphaned upgrades still draining). Subtracting it, a single capture costs the same in both (24.2 energy a tick, 750 =
the generator's `BuildCostEnergy` over 31 ticks).
