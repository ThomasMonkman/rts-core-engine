rate differs by more than 0.3 rad/s) and leg times agree (ours / real median 0.98 / 0.92 / 1.01 / 1.06 over the four

Gameplay effect today: every hover / amphibious unit with a `WaterSpeedMultiplier` and every sub with a `SubSpeedMultiplier` is 5.5..10% faster in ours than in the real game. Real orders start 3-4 ticks later throughout (intended latency, not counted). Speeds are `10 x |GetVelocity|` xz.
